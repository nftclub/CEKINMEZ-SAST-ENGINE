## 🚫 The Problem: The "Context Window" Trap
Modern security teams rely on WAFs and standard LLMs (Large Language Models), but both have fatal flaws:
* **WAFs:** Only see surface-level anomalies. They cannot understand complex business logic.
* **LLMs:** Suffer from "Context Window" limits. They lose track of project architecture after 5-10 files. You cannot secure a 60,000-file ecosystem with a tool that forgets the middleware by the time it audits the controller.

## ⚡ The Solution: Unified Memory Pool & Deterministic Tracing
CEKINMEZ loads your **entire architecture** into a single Unified Memory Pool. It doesn't guess; it determines.

* **Zero Token Limits:** Analyze 6,000 or 60,000 files with the same surgical precision.
* **Cross-File Logic Hunting:** Tracks tainted data across dozens of modules, from the router to the deepest database sink.
* **Zero Noise:** We don't provide 2,000-page reports of missing semicolons. We identify the **"tip of the thread"**—the exact intersection where your logic breaks.
